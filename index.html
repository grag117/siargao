Our story

More than a destination. A feeling.

Feel and Discover Siargao is an original tourism business concept created to help travelers experience the natural beauty, adventure, and welcoming island culture of Siargao, Philippines.

We imagine journeys that go beyond taking photos: exploring with curiosity, appreciating local communities, and leaving the island with meaningful memories. This project promotes responsible tourism and thoughtful travel planning.

Our Mission

To offer memorable, convenient, and responsible travel experiences while celebrating Siargao's natural resources, culture, and local communities.

Our Vision

To become a trusted island-tour brand known for warm hospitality, enjoyable experiences, and a commitment to sustainable tourism.

💚Respect NatureHelp protect the island environment.
🤝Warm HospitalityWelcome every traveler with care.
🌊Real ExperiencesCelebrate local places and culture.
✨Guest FirstMake every trip thoughtful and enjoyable.

Our target market: Local and international tourists, students, couples, families, and adventure seekers.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers to help you prepare for your Siargao adventure.

Ask about a tour →
How do I reserve a tour?

Complete the inquiry form above. In this demo, your email app opens with a prepared message. A real reservation needs a working business email or connected form service and confirmation from the operator.

Are the prices final?

No. The displayed prices are sample rates for this student website project. Actual prices, fees, transport, meals, and availability must be confirmed before booking.

What should I bring?

Bring sun protection, swimwear, a towel, drinking water in a reusable bottle, and a waterproof bag. Check local weather and tour conditions before departure.

Can you arrange a custom trip?

Choose “Custom Island Experience” in the inquiry form and describe your interests, preferred date, and group size.

How can I help protect Siargao?

Follow local guidelines, avoid leaving litter, respect wildlife and communities, and choose responsible local services where possible.
